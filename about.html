Our story

Marcus Reyes started Tideline Plumbing in 2014 out of the back of a pickup truck, after years working for a larger company where jobs felt rushed and customers felt like numbers. The idea was simple: show up on time, explain what's actually wrong, and charge a fair price for fixing it right the first time.

Eleven years later, Tideline is still family-run, still local to Rivermill, and still answers the phone when it rings — even at 2am.
